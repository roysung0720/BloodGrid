# Demo Plan

1. Show the rural region with three Blood Response Units, candidate staging locations, hospitals, and demand-proxy incidents.
2. Show the initial modeled 20-minute coverage percentage.
3. Run strategic deployment optimization and show unit moves plus improved coverage.
4. Simulate a major motor-vehicle collision with a selected destination trauma center.
5. Compare direct transport with the recommended meeting spot on the way (e.g. a gas station or parking lot): time-to-blood and added hospital delay, and why other units were not chosen.
6. Mark the recommended resource out of service and recalculate to demonstrate that BloodGrid responds to operational change.

The presenter should state that recommendations are logistics support after a blood request, not clinical treatment decisions.
